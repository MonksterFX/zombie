# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09.March 2026

## Overview

Zombie - Battery Monitor ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

The App is designed to function without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal data.

## Information Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information about users.

Specifically, the App does not:

- Collect personal identifiers (such as name, email address, or phone number)
- Track location
- Access contacts, photos, or files
- Use analytics services
- Use advertising identifiers
- Track usage behavior
- Require user accounts

All functionality is performed locally on your device.

## Data Storage

The App does not store personal data.

> If the App saves preferences or configuration settings, these are stored locally on your device using standard macOS system storage and are not transmitted to any external servers.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

## Network Access

The App does not transmit any user data over the internet.

> If future versions introduce features that require network access, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from users, including children under the age of 13.
